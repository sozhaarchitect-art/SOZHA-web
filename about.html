Our Studio

Established in 2020, Sozha has quickly emerged as a leading voice in contemporary design & build. Our studio is a collaborative space where designers, interior specialists, and engineers work together to push the boundaries of design.

We do not just build structures; we curate lifestyles. Whether it is a luxury residence or a high-traffic commercial hub, our approach is rooted in understanding the human element of every space.
